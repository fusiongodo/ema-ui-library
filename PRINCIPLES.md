# Design principles

The yardstick for every screen. If a design breaks one of these, it's wrong, however good it looks.

1. **Effort is the scarcest resource.** Every screen answers: what is the least the user has to do here?
2. **Neutral = as planned.** Defaults are invisible. Only deviations get marks or color.
3. **Show only what isn't default.** "Daily" and "1 entry" are not printed.
4. **Configuration lives where it is used.** Adding or changing opens a sheet over the current view, never a separate settings hop.
5. **Voice is the shortcut, taps the fallback.** The AI only writes drafts; nothing is saved without confirmation.
6. **History is versioned, never overwritten.** Edits apply from today; past days keep their version.
7. **Calm over busy.** One primary action per screen. Readable text never shrinks.
