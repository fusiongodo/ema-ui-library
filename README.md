# design/

- `PRINCIPLES.md`: the rules every screen is checked against
- `wireframes.html`: open in a browser (double-click), three views: Library, Flows, Play
- `screens/<area>/<name>.js`: one file per screen, structure only
- `flows/<name>.js`: one file per user flow (start screen only, the rest is derived)
- `lib/`: renderer (`render.js`), flow map (`flows.js`), player (`play.js`), router (`app.js`), `style.css`, `themes.css` (Play themes), `cozy.css` + `cozy.js` (Cozy theme), `studio.css` (Studio theme), `buddy.js` (Cozy companion), `splash.js` + `splash.css` (intro)
- `refs/`: inspiration screenshots (Juno, Whoop, own ChatGPT mockups). Mood only, never the spec.

No build step, no npm. Plain JS, works from `file://`.

## Views
Opening the page without a hash starts **Play** on the first flow. The pill at the top (or `••• Menu` in the phone's status bar on a small screen) switches views.
- **Library**: every screen, Current vs. Proposed per area. Tap a phone (or `▶ play`) to start Play on that screen.
- **Flows**: one card per flow. Screens left to right in tap order, branches as extra rows, arrows start at the tapped element and carry its label. `‹ back`, `× close` and loops (`↻`) are only marked under a screen, not drawn. Tap a screen to play from it.
- **Play**: one clickable phone. Sheets slide up over the dimmed screen below. Tapping an unlinked spot flashes all hotspots. Folds, toggles, pills, segments, choices and sliders react locally (visual only). `‹ Back`, `↺ Restart`, `Flows`, `Library` sit above the phone; on a phone they open from `••• Menu`. Play draws the screens in iOS proportions (17 pt body, 34 pt large titles, 44 pt tap targets, 16 pt gutters, tab bar and sheet buttons pinned to the bottom; `PLAY · iOS scale` in `lib/style.css`), while Library and Flows keep the compact preview scale.

**Themes (Play only).** `••• Menu` / the bar above the phone has `Theme` (Cozy · Studio · Sky · Dusk · Meadow · Wire) and, for Cozy and Sky, `Time` (Auto · Morning · Noon · Evening · Night; Auto follows the clock). Default: Cozy, Auto. The choice is kept in `localStorage` when the browser allows it. Library and Flows always stay grey wireframes; **Wire** is the plain prototype.
- **Cozy** (default): friendly and soft, like a cozy game. Every area has its own colour world: a flat pastel page, a dark variant for titles, primary buttons and the active tab, a very light variant for icon tiles. Today apricot `#FFD49B` / `#8A3F0A` / `#FFF2DF`, Check-ins mint `#BDE5CC` / `#1B5C40` / `#E6F6EC`, Protocols (with all add / group / plan screens and sheets) periwinkle `#C5CBF7` / `#383D9C` / `#ECEEFE`, Benchmarks + Insights lilac rosé `#EDC8E2` / `#782C66` / `#FBEEF7`, Voice deep teal `#2F6B6A` (light text). The world comes from the screen's area (or its `world`); a sheet takes the world of its own area, not of the screen below (the check-in sheet is mint over Today too); the page colour blends over ~400 ms on a tab change. Colour = place (PRINCIPLES 9): every link into another area wears that area's world wherever it sits (on Today the Check-in card and the Check-ins tile are mint, the Protocols tile periwinkle, the Play tiles lilac rosé; the page, its title and tab bar stay apricot), the mic in the tab bar is Voice teal `#2F6B6A` on every page, and scores are mint wherever they are drawn as a line. White cards with 28 pt corners, Fredoka for titles and numbers, Nunito for text (Google Fonts; offline the rounded system font), round cartoon icons instead of the emoji and the grey tab placeholders, a floating pill tab bar with the mic as a round button in the middle, a centred page head with round buttons (a `+ Add` section action becomes the round primary `+` in the dark world colour), a short squish on press. A small companion (a person, `lib/buddy.js`) sits next to the Today greeting (awake with a mug by day, asleep at night) and in empty states (asleep). Quiet motion, one or two things per page: two clouds on Today, the pulse icon on Check-ins, the sun of the protocol folds, the dice on Benchmarks; it holds still while a sheet is open. Save sends up a few small sparks. The tinted card (PRINCIPLES 8) is the dark world colour. Styles: `lib/cozy.css`; Play calls `Cozy.decorate()` (`lib/cozy.js`) on each painted layer, screen files stay as they are. Sliders are **pill sliders** (as in Studio): the track is the light variant of the area, the fill uses Studio's area colours (`--cz-fill` = Studio's `--st-fill`) with the same gradient, glow and pale untouched state.
  The companion is a placeholder from simple shapes. To use finished art, put one image per state into `BUDDY_ART` in `lib/buddy.js` (`sleep`, `awake`, `listening`, `speaking`); nothing else changes.
  The listening layer (half-height sheets of the Voice area) is the Voice world itself: deep teal, light text, white draft cards with the edge and icon tile of their area, a white primary button. Review cards are white with the icon tile of their area; `heard` rows carry the light tint of that area and a small teal mic before the quote; an `unsure` value is a grey, dashed suggestion (no tint). A filled-in slider (`heard`) shows its words in muted italics beside the value; the moved fill already marks it. Scores keep the Check-ins mint on Today as well (icon tile and curves of "Scores · 7 days").
- **Studio**: a calm, neutral stage. Almost white page `#FCFCFB`, light warm-grey cards `#F3F2EF` without borders or shadows (28 pt corners, rows and lists 24), lots of air, system sans (SF Pro / Inter: titles 34 bold, sections 22 bold, numbers big and bold). The main action is a full-width near-black pill (`#141416`), secondary actions light-grey pills, a round black `+` in the head where a screen adds something; chips and pills are white on a grey card; segmented controls a grey track with a white segment; sheets white with a 32 pt top radius. Colour = place moves into the icon tiles: every icon sits in a pastel rounded square (16 pt) of its area, with a soft highlight and a light, dimensional icon (Cozy's shapes, white bodies, lines in the area colour, a small drop shadow). Today apricot, Check-ins mint, Protocols periwinkle, Insights lilac rosé, Voice teal, `neutral` warm grey. The area colour also appears in the active tab (a grey capsule in the white floating tab bar), in slider / progress / data fills and as the pale tint of the one tinted card (with a black button inside); the mic in the tab bar is teal on every page. Evaluation glyphs are black (filled = better, ring = worse). The companion sits on a pastel circle; its small breathing / blinking stays, Cozy's floating clouds, squish and sparks do not (Studio uses the soft themes' ring of light). No Time choice (the companion follows the clock), no dark appearance. Built as a skin: Play calls `Cozy.decorate()` for Studio too (icons, heads, `data-world` on layers and links), and `lib/studio.css` restyles that DOM; none of `cozy.css` applies. The palette is two blocks of variables at the top of `studio.css` (per area: `--st-tile`/`-hi`/`-lo`, `--st-ink`, `--st-fill`, `--st-data`, `--st-tint`; the stage: `--st-page`, `--st-card`, `--st-black` …). Sliders are **pill sliders** (`lib/themes.css`): the question, a centred big value "5/10", a 44 pt capsule track with one dot per step, a gradient fill in the area colour, from a touch lighter than `--st-fill` (85 % with white) to 60 % with white (with a soft glow once moved) that ends under a 36 pt white knob, the end names below; an untouched slider has a pale fill and a muted number, the last value is a darker dot instead of a tick (on the fill a larger white dot).
- **Sky**: the background is a sky that follows the time of day (the same morning / noon / evening / night split as the protocol folds), slow clouds, milk-glass cards, glass tab bar and sheets. Accent: sunlight yellow by day, moon white at night.
- **Dusk**: dark and low-stimulus, a barely moving aurora, cards one step lighter than the ground, numbers and curves glow faintly. Accent: muted amber. Least motion.
- **Meadow**: sage mist to sand, a calm field of soft colour (three edgeless washes of sage, sand and a whisper of terracotta) that drifts and breathes slowly, no shapes; opaque warm-white cards. Accent: terracotta, only on the primary tile.

Sky, Dusk, Meadow: rounded type for titles and numbers, 24 pt card radius, depth from soft shadows instead of lines, cards rise slightly (staggered) on a screen change, a soft glow on a dragged slider thumb, one ring of light after a sheet's final button (Save, Add to …). Motion is transform/opacity only and switches off with `prefers-reduced-motion`. A theme is a set of CSS variables on the phone frame (`data-theme`, `data-tod`, class `soft`), see `lib/themes.css`; PRINCIPLES 8 still holds (one tinted tile, the theme only decides its colour). The Today greeting (`head` with `greet:true`) follows the same time of day.

Navigation is in-memory (a stack of screen ids), not the URL: no hash changes, no `hashchange`, no `history.go`, so it also works when the bundled page runs inside a sandboxed iframe. The browser's own back button therefore does not step through Play. As a convenience the current place is mirrored into the hash (`#library/<area>`, `#flows/<flow-id>`, `#play/<flow-id>/<screen-id>`) where the browser allows it, and a hash present on load is opened once as a deep link.

`./bundle.sh` writes everything into one page fragment, `.dist/ema-wireframes.html`, for sharing / phone review.

## Add a screen
1. Copy an existing file in `screens/<area>/`, change `id`, `title`, `status`, `w`.
2. Add one `<script src="screens/<area>/<name>.js">` line at the bottom of `wireframes.html`.

`sheet:true` = opens as a sheet over the screen it was opened from. `sheet:"half"` = a half-height sheet (≈ 58 % of the screen): the page above stays visible, dimmed, so you never lose your place; the content scrolls inside it, trailing buttons stay pinned. Library and Flows draw it lower, under a grey band that stands for the page. In Play a tap on the dimmed page closes a half sheet (a full sheet only flashes the hotspots); a full sheet stays visible under a half sheet opened from it.

`after:[ms, "id"]` (screen, Play only) = moves on to `id` by itself after `ms`, replacing the screen (a passing state, so Back does not return to it). A tap anywhere on the screen moves on at once; touching it first (a slider) only stops the clock. With reduced motion there is no clock, only the tap. In Flows the link is drawn from the phone, labelled `auto`. Used by the listening layer (`voice/listen` → `voice/drafts`) and the guided questions.

`of:"voice/drafts"` (screen, Play only) = this screen is a later state of that one (`voice/drafts-1` = the drafts with the check-in saved). A link to it returns to where `voice/drafts` sits in the stack and replaces it: from a sheet on top, the sheet slides down and the state fades in; from the screen itself, it fades in place. Cozy keeps the page's colour on a button that links to an `of` screen (Save reads like close, not like opening Voice). `of` may name the screen itself (`trials/helper`): a link to it then returns to where it already stands in the stack instead of stacking a copy (the helper preview's × and "Back to your setup").

## Link screens: `go`
Put the target screen id on the tapped widget. `"back"` = one step back, `"close"` = close all open sheets.

| Widget | Property |
|---|---|
| `btn`, `tile`, `fold`, `mic`, `field`, `draft`, `note`, `sec` (no `act`), any other single widget | `go:"protocols/add-0-choose"` |
| `sec` with `act` | `go` = the act button |
| `tile` with `act` | `act` draws a button inside the tile; `go` = that button |
| `head` | `go` = the right circle |
| `shead` | `goLeft`, `goRight` |
| `seg` | `go` per item, `goRight` = the link on the right |
| `chips`, `pills`, `choice`, `list`, `tl`, `seg`, chips inside a `tile` | `go:[ "id", null, "id" ]` parallel to the items (`null` = not tappable), or one string for all |
| `fold` rows (`open`) | `goRows:[ "id", null ]` parallel to `open` (a row opens its protocol; the fold header still folds) |
| `plist` | 4th element of a row; 3rd element of a group's item |
| `review` | 4th element of a row (`unsure`: the tap that confirms the assumption); `ask` = "Ask me instead"; `compact:true`: `go` = the whole card (none with `ok`) |
| `list`, `tl` | `info:[ "id", null ]` parallel to the rows = an info icon after the row's title (its own tap target, the row keeps its `go`) |
| `sec` | `info:"id"` = an info icon after the heading |

A `tile` with `go` as an array links its chips; as a string, the whole tile. `pills` with `multi:true` allow several selections in Play. A `seg` item (Day ↔ Week) swaps the screen in Play: it fades like a tab change and replaces the screen instead of stacking it, so Back skips it.

Tab bars link via `TABLINKS` in `lib/render.js` (tab name → screen id, per tabset). Tab links are ignored when building flows. A screen can override them with `tabto:{Protocols:"protocols/today-folds-trial"}`: the prototype keeps no state, so a state of the app (a running trial) is carried by its screens, and their tabs lead to the matching states of the other tabs.

Broken targets are logged to the console on load and shown as `⚠` in the flow map.

## Widget extras
- `head`: `n` = small line under the title (Today: the date); `greet:true` = in Play the title becomes "Good morning / afternoon / evening" for the current time of day.
- Screen (optional, Cozy only): `sub` = small status line under the page title ("All as planned · evening next"); `world` = colour world when it differs from the area (`today`, `checkins`, `protocols`, `insights`, `voice`; Benchmarks uses `insights`; `neutral` = warm grey, belongs to no area: the trial helper's pages, another person's view).
- `tile`: `cta:true` = a round arrow on the right, the whole tile reads as one button (Today's "Check-in now"). A `tone` other than `white` / `grey` gets class `tint`. `r:"›"` with `go` = a quiet link card; Cozy lays it out as one row (icon tile, name with `n` below, › at the end), e.g. "All protocols" at the end of the Protocols tab.
- `tile` `spark` rows: `["Name","value"]` draws the dashed placeholder; `["Name","value",[6,7,5,6,4,5,6]]` draws a small curve from the values (oldest → today, fixed 0–10 scale so rows compare, dashed midline at 5, today's point emphasised).

## Info icon and explanations
A proper term is named as what it is and explained on demand, not paraphrased: a small (i) right after it opens a half-height sheet with a picture and one or two sentences. Word budget (the people using the app have ME/CFS; reading is exhausting): at most two short sentences, about 20 to 25 words, plain words, the term used once; what needs more goes into the picture, or goes. The same holds for the guide's cards.
- `info` on `list` / `tl` (parallel to the rows) and on `sec` (one id) draws the icon (`info()` in `lib/render.js`, an inline SVG, so every theme shows it; Cozy draws it in the dark world colour). It is its own tap target inside a tappable row: the row still opens its picker. 44 pt hit area in Play. In Flows its links rank last (`LINK_PRI.info`), so they branch off and never become the spine; their label is "About <term>".
- `list` row = `[label, right, style, sub]`: `sub` = a small line under the label (a choice with its meaning, `trials/measure`).
- `explain`: one explanation card, `{t:"explain", pic:"washout", l:"Title" (optional), n:"One or two sentences."}`. A picture from `XP` in `lib/render.js` (inline SVG, 320 wide, `currentColor`; real = filled, placebo = ring, washout = faint, veiled = pale, the code of the trial's daystrip) above the text, in a white card. Pictures: `washout`, `block`, `randomized`, `placebo`, `blinded`, `primary`, `composite` (terms) and `prep-fill`, `prep-nest`, `prep-match`, `prep-sachet` (the trial guide). A new term = one entry in `XP` and one sheet.
- The sheet: one kind for every term, `trialTerm()` in `screens/trials/terms.js`: `sheet:"half"`, the term as the title, × back, one `explain` card without `l` (the sentence then reads in body size). The trial's terms: `trials/term-placebo`, `-blinded`, `-randomized`, `-block`, `-washout`, `-primary`, `-composite`; their texts are `TRIAL_TERMS`, which the guide reuses with the same pictures.

## Review card and voice widgets
The manual way and the voice agent end in the same overview: one **review card** per action. The agent only skips the steps in between; nothing is saved without the card's button (PRINCIPLES 5).
- `review`: `{t:"review", title:"New protocol", n:"small line", rows:[[label, value, state, go, quote]]}`. Label left, value right; a row with `go` opens the step that fills it (chevron). `state`: `""` normal · `"default"` the default value, drawn muted (PRINCIPLES 2) · `"heard"` filled in by the agent: a calm tint and the words below in quotes (`quote`), no blinking · `"unsure"` not said, only assumed: `quote` is the reason under the label ("You didn't say how often"), `value` the assumption as a grey, dashed suggestion, no chevron; its `go` is the tap that confirms it (`protocols/add-review-heard` → `-2`) · `"empty"` still open: `value` is the muted prompt. The card states values, it is not a form: one value per row, the choices open after a tap in a half-height picker (`protocols/pick-when` for one or more times, `pick-rhythm`, `pick-start`, `pick-end`). A `value` can still be a widget (`{t:"pills", …}`, drawn full width under the label), but no review card uses that any more: unselected pills read as open questions. No mic inside a row: to say a missing part, the sheet has its own centred `mic` under the card (`protocols/add-review-heard`), with "Ask me instead" as a quiet `note` with `go` right under it. The card's closing button is the screen's trailing `btn` (pinned at the bottom in Play); while an `unsure` row is unconfirmed it is `off:true` (greyed out, no `go`), so nothing is saved as a guess. `icon` (Cozy) overrides the area icon (`"stack"` for a group). `tone:"neutral"` = belongs to no area (a note).
- `review` with `compact:true`: the folded form for the listening layer, `{t:"review", compact:true, title:"Check-in", n:"Fatigue 7 · Brain fog 6 · Pain 3 · rest unchanged", go:"checkins/entry-heard"}`. Title and one summary line; the whole card opens the full overview. In Cozy it is white with the edge and icon tile of its area (from its `go` target, PRINCIPLES 9), neutral grey with `tone:"neutral"`. In Play compact cards appear one after another (none with reduced motion).
- `review` extras (the agent's certainty, never a gate): `clarity:"clear"|"partly"|"cloudy"` = a small sky in the card head (sun · sun behind a cloud · cloud) for how much was said vs. worked out (used on the voice draft cards and `protocols/deviation-heard`; not on the add-protocol review, where the grey unconfirmed row already says what is open); a tap shows one line below the head (`hint`, or a default per value: "Everything was said clearly" · "Part of it was worked out, not said" · "Most of it was guessed, please check"). Data-wise an agent value carries `inferred:true` when it was not said; the card only sums it up. `ok:true` (compact) = already saved: muted, a ✓ instead of the chevron, not tappable. `ask:"protocols/add-ask"` = a quiet "Ask me instead" link at the card's end (the agent asks for the open rows, one half sheet each). `live:[1000,1500,null,…]` parallel to `rows` (Play) = the row fills in after that many ms (dots until then; `null` = there from the start); the card builds up while you speak (`protocols/add-listen`).
- `slider` extras: `set` = a value already filled in (drawn moved, the tick stays at `v`), `heard` = the words it came from, shown beside the value (“pretty wiped” → 7). Moving it by hand replaces the quote with "was N". A slider nobody has touched (no `set`) draws its title and value greyed (contrast ≥ 4.5); the first touch brings both forward.
- `btn` with `slots:true` (Play): the text names the chosen times and follows them ("Add to Morning + Noon"; "Add" when none): pills in the review card itself, or the pills of a picker sheet opened from a row (`protocols/pick-when`), which also write the row's value ("Morning + Noon"). A picker finds its row by the row's `go` (= the picker's id).
- `btn` with `off:true`: greyed out and not tappable (no `go`); used while something on the card is still open (`protocols/add-review-heard`).
- 🎙 in any text or label is drawn as an inline SVG mic icon (no emoji on screen).
- `say`: one line of transcript in quotes, `{t:"say", l:"pretty wiped today…", live:true, d:1300}`; `live` = the words arrive one by one in Play (`d` = start delay in ms; reduced motion: all at once). A `slider` or `pills` right after a live `say` appears once the answer is in.
- `bud`: the companion in a screen, `{t:"bud", st:"listening"}` (large, centred) or with `l:"How is your energy right now?"` (small figure, the spoken line beside it). States: `listening` (eyes closed, a slight nod), `speaking` (eyes open, the mouth moves a few times, then rests), `awake`, `sleep`. Cozy draws the figure (`lib/buddy.js`), Library / Wire a grey placeholder.
- `chips` with `quiet:true`: small actions spread across one line (the listening layer's "Ask me instead" and ×); a chip without `go` is plain text ("1 of 2").

**Voice model.** Speak first, a conversation only when needed. One agent, two doors: the mic in the tab bar (global) opens the half-height listening layer over the current page (`voice/listen`), works out what was meant and shows compact draft cards (`voice/drafts`); a mic inside a form (local) fills that form: in Add protocol it opens a listening sheet where the card builds up while you speak (`protocols/add-listen` → `add-review-heard`), in the check-in it fills the sliders directly (`checkins/entry-heard-all`). Saving one draft returns to the drafts with that card ticked (`voice/drafts-1`, `-2`); "Confirm all" stays. Either way the result is the overview of the manual way. `voice/voice` is the earlier full-page idea, kept in the Library, no longer linked.

## Protocol history widgets
- `plist`: list of protocols. `rows:[[name, sub, days, go, kids]]`. `sub` = only what isn't default (dose, slot, "day 4 of 14"). `days` = the last 14 days, today last: `x` taken, `h` smaller dose, `o` planned but skipped (ring), `.` not planned (small dot); `""` = no strip. `kids:[[name, sub, go]]` makes the row a group that unfolds (Play starts folded, Library shows it open). An "Ended" section is one group row.
- `cal`: intake calendar, Monday to Sunday, newest week at the bottom, month label where a week starts in a new month. `{t:"cal", from:"2026-09-14", key:{x:["6 mg",1], s:["4 mg instead of 6 mg",.67,"4 mg"]}, weeks:[".....x.", …, ".."], idle:"no intake"}`. `from` = the Monday of the first week (ISO date); one string per week, the last character of the last week is today, missing days after it are the future. A `key` letter = taken: `[tap label, size relative to the usual dose, legend label or false]`; `o` = skipped; `-` = planned for later today (faint); `.` = not planned (`idle` names it in the legend and on tap). Legend in one line from what occurs. Play: a tapped day names itself under the grid ("Sun 25 Oct · 6 mg"), visual only; day cells are ≥ 40 pt.
  - Several doses a day: `doses:["morning","noon","evening"]`, and each week is one token per day, separated by spaces, one character per dose: `weeks:["hhh hoh hhh …", …, "xxx xx-"]`. The dot is split into one segment per dose, left to right (all taken = a full dot, as quiet as a plain one; a skipped dose = a hollow segment; a dose smaller than the others that day = a paler segment; the dot size follows the day's dose as before). The legend starts with a small split dot and the dose names. A tap names every dose: "Sat 31 Oct · morning ✓ · noon skipped · evening ✓" (a key with size 1 is ✓, any other key says its label; a day taken entirely at a smaller dose says it once: "Thu 17 Sep · 0.5 mg · morning ✓ · noon skipped · evening ✓"). Once-a-day items leave out `doses` and keep the plain dot (`protocols/item-rapamycin`, `protocols/item-magnesium`); `protocols/item-naltrexon` uses it.
  - `gap:true` = one line under the calendar names the most repeated gap, counted from the grid: the dose and weekday missed most often, at least 3 times ("Noon dose missed on 3 of 7 Saturdays."; without `doses`: "Missed on …"). Nothing repeats: no line. A string instead of `true` is shown as is.
  - `change:[["2026-10-12","now 1 mg (was 0.5 mg)"]]` = the day a new dose starts: a short dashed line at that day's left edge (the chart's "dose changed" line), "dose changed" in the legend, the text in the day's tap line. Without it a jump in dot size between two rows reads like a drawing glitch (`protocols/item-naltrexon`). Split days use class `cs` (not `ds`, the daystrip's card).
- `stats`: small numbers in one card (three by default, any count lays out in one row), `items:[["5×","in 8 weeks"], …]`.
- `linemarks`: one score over the same weeks with marks. `{t:"linemarks", from:"2026-09-14", n:"last 8 weeks · marks = intake", series:[["Fatigue","6776…"], …], on:0, marks:["2026-09-19", …]}`. Each series = one character per day from `from` to today (`0`–`9`, `a` = 10, `.` = no value), fixed 0–10 scale. `marks`: an ISO date draws a thin line with a dot on top (intake); `["2026-10-12","dose changed"]` a dashed, labelled line. The chips above switch the series in Play. Neutral wording only: the chart puts things side by side, it claims no effect. Extras (Insights): `avg:["2026-10-12"]` splits the weeks at these dates and draws a flat, labelled line at each part's average (the data line steps back; follows the chips); `q:"Did the dose change help?"` = a question as the card's title (a pinned answer); `say:"…"` = one sentence under the chart, inside the card.

## Insights widgets
The Insights tab (`insights/home`) has three parts: Patterns (found by the app), Ask (the agent picks a built analysis, the numbers come from that function; "Pin to Insights" puts the answer on the tab), Trials (a personal trial, see below). Wording is always "goes along with", never "causes".
- `pattern`: one finding in one sentence. `{t:"pattern", src:"protocols", l:"After a skipped noon dose of Naltrexon, your fatigue, pain and brain fog were worse the same evening.", n:"Seen 6 times", conf:"likely", go:"insights/pattern-skip"}`. The card says what happened in words; numbers come after the tap. Without `conf` it can also name an evaluation ("Your Rapamycin evaluation", `n:"6 things were different in the two days after"`). `conf` = `hint` (seen a few times, could be chance) · `likely` (holds over weeks) · `strong`, drawn as a quiet word. `src` = the area the finding comes from: Cozy draws the icon tile in that area's world (PRINCIPLES 9), the card stays white. The icon itself is Insights' own (`finding`: a rising line with a small spark), so a finding about a protocol does not look like a Protocols item; `ic:"overview"` = the sibling icon for an evaluation card ("Your Rapamycin evaluation"). Themes without icons keep the plain grey tile. `big:true` = the evidence screen's title (the sentence large, the word and `n` as one line below, no card).
- `compare`: two groups of days on the same 0–10 scale, one dot per day, a bar at each average, the difference big in the head. `{t:"compare", l:"Fatigue", n:"the same evening after a skip vs. other evenings", a:{l:"Evenings after a skip", v:[8,7,7,8,8,7], d:["Thu 17 Sep · noon skipped", …]}, b:{l:"Other evenings", v:"7787…"}}`. `a` = few days, large dots; in Play a tapped dot names its day and value below. `b` = many days as a digit string (`a` = 10, `.` = none), small dots three to a row at each value. Averages and difference are computed from the values. Inline SVG; in Cozy the dots and bars are scores (mint). `b` can also be a few values like `a` (`v:[6.6,5.8,…]`, `d:["Week 1", …]`): large tappable dots too (the trial's week averages). Close values step up instead of overlapping. `tap` = the hint under the chart (default "Tap one of the N days").
- Score switch on an evidence screen: a `seg` above the `compare` (`items:["Fatigue","Pain","Brain fog"]`), each item linking to a sibling state of the same page (`insights/pattern-skip-pain`, `-fog`, `of:"insights/pattern-skip"`). The segment swaps the state in place, Back leads to where the page was opened from.
- `diffs` extras: a group without `l` draws its rows without a head (equals, no Better / Worse split: the trial result's "Each score"); `dir:"same"` = about the same, a dash in a plain ring.
- `diffs`: an evaluation, everything that was different around one kind of event, in one card. `{t:"diffs", l:"Two days after a dose", n:"vs. all other days …", strip:{from:"2026-09-14", n:51, marks:["2026-09-19", …], after:2, key:["dose","two days after"]}, groups:[{l:"Better", rows:[["Pain","0.8","lower","down","checkins","likely"], …]}, {l:"Worse", worse:true, rows:[…]}]}`. `strip` = the days as a row of dots (a ring on each event, the `after` days filled, months below, a one-line key). Row = `[name, amount, word, dir, src, conf]`: amount big and word small at the end, `dir` (`up` / `down`) = an arrow for which way the measure went, `src` = a quiet source label (`checkins` → "Check-in", mint in Cozy; `health` → "Apple Health", neutral grey), `conf` = the pattern's quiet word. Better rows have a filled circle, worse rows a ring, each group under its own head, so the two never depend on colour. Used by `insights/eval-rapamycin`.
- `more`: a quiet line of actions under a screen. `{t:"more", l:"Details", act:"Run a trial", goAct:null, rows:[["Evenings after a skip","6"], …], n:"Method: …"}`. `l` unfolds the rows and the sentence in place (Play starts folded, Library shows them open); `act` is a second quiet link (`goAct` = its target).

## Trial widgets
The personal placebo-controlled trial (flows `trial`, `trial-helper`; screens in `screens/trials/`, BACKLOG "Personal placebo-controlled trial"). The app only provides the structure and honest information; the person designs the trial. Step 2 `trials/measure`: the scores to track and how the result is judged, the primary outcome: a composite score of all of them, one score, or none (all side by side), three states of one screen (`-one`, `-none`, `of`). Step 3 `trials/length`: block duration, washout period, total duration, each with a half-height picker and a custom entry. Proper terms carry an info icon (see "Info icon and explanations"). A block is exact and counts only the compared days; every block is preceded by its washout days, switch or not (capsule days of the kind that follows), so one block takes washout + block days and the total is rounded up to whole blocks (demo: 8 weeks → 7 × 9 = 63 days). The app draws the order of the blocks (with an odd number one kind gets one more, also drawn) and where the first one begins (the sequence shifted, one block split between start and end), and keeps all of it hidden until the end: one numbered capsule a day, no visible rhythm. Step 4 `trials/helper` states the rule of blinding (`trialRule()` in `trials/intro.js`: you can't tell them apart, only the helper knows, each carries only a number), not a recipe. The optional guide, `trials/guide` (what a blinded trial is, the rule) → `trials/guide-prepare` (ways to prepare, as examples), is offered on `trials/intro`, on the helper step (straight to the examples) and as a small line in the Trials section of every Insights state (`INSIGHTS_GUIDE`); both pages go back to where they were opened. The demo plan is `TRIAL_PLAN` in `trials/intro.js`: `[[from, to, "a"|"b", role?], …]`, role none = block days, `"w"` = washout. Setup sheets over the Insights tab end in a `review` card; the Insights tab has four more states for it (`insights/home-trial`, `home-result`, `home-trial-pinned`, `home-stopped`, all `of:"insights/home"`), the Protocols tab one (`protocols/today-folds-trial`), All protocols one (`protocols/all-trial`, `of:"protocols/all"`). Under Protocols Magnesium itself is the trial item: one row, its name with a small calm "Trial" badge (`TRIAL_BADGE` in `trials/intro.js`, class `trb`, `data-world="insights"`) and tonight's trial capsule number ("capsule no. 19"), nothing paused, no separate capsule; its detail in the trial state is `protocols/item-capsule` (today's capsule number, taken / skipped since the start, what to do after a missed day, "Trial status ›" to `trials/running`). What one swallows is always a "trial capsule". Doing lives in Protocols, understanding in Insights: while the trial runs (`insights/home-trial`, `trials/running` and the three Protocols screens) the tabs use `tabto`, so Protocols leads to the trial state of Protocols and Insights back to the trial state of Insights. The helper's pages use `world:"neutral"` and no tab bar; `trials/peek-*` show them to the person as a preview, derived from the helper's own screens (`trialPeek()` in `peek-welcome.js`).
- `qr`: a QR code placeholder (fixed pattern with the three corner squares, not a real code) in a white card. `{t:"qr", l:"Sam scans this", n:"Opens the fill plan on Sam's phone."}`.
- `fill`: the helper's fill plan. `{t:"fill", ranges:TRIAL_PLAN, a:"Magnesium 300 mg", b:"Placebo", unit:"capsules", done:2}`: `ranges` = `[[from, to, "a"|"b", role?], …]` (the plan), the numbered units as runs ("1–3 Magnesium 300 mg", "4–21 Placebo"; neighbouring entries of one kind are merged into one run), one check-off per run, totals below (as they fall, 27 / 36 in the demo); `unit` = what a number is (default "compartments"; the trial uses "capsules"). Older form: `rows:"BAAB…"` = one box per week of `per` capsules. `done` = runs already ticked off. Play: a tap ticks one off (visual only).
- `daystrip`: every day of the trial as one dot, 28 to a line, day numbers at the line ends. `{t:"daystrip", veil:true, days:63, l:"…"}` = the person's side: all days alike, no block boundary, no weekday. `{t:"daystrip", plan:TRIAL_PLAN, key:["Magnesium","Placebo","washout"]}` = the reveal after the end: real days filled, placebo days a ring, washout days (role `"w"`) faint in their kind, each in the key only where it occurs (older plans without roles: `skip:n` = the first n days of each entry faint); `upto:19` cuts it (stopped early). `l` = one sentence under the strip. `sum:["8 weeks → 63 days", "7 blocks × (2 washout + 7 days)"]` = the arithmetic as a sum taken in at a glance, the result over its formula, between the strip and `l` (`trials/length`).
- `tl` with `ic:["capsule","box","pulse"]` (parallel to rows): a small picture tile instead of the dot and no label column; the drawings come from Cozy's icon set (`lib/cozy.js`: also `twins`, `key`, `hash`), plain ink in Library and the other themes, the world colours in Cozy. Used for the rule of blinding on `trials/helper` and `trials/guide`.

## Slider
`{t:"slider", l:"Pain", v:3, min:0, max:10, lo:"0", hi:"10", better:"low"}`: `v` = the last value (where the slider starts), `lo`/`hi` = names of the ends (default: `min`/`max`), `better` = which end is good (data only, not drawn). Library and Flows draw it static and muted. In Play a tap sets the value and a horizontal drag moves it (44 pt touch strip, `touch-action:pan-y`, so a vertical swipe still scrolls the sheet); a touched slider turns strong and shows a tick and "was N" at the last value. In Studio and Cozy (pill slider) a tap or drag snaps to the nearest step, a tap jump eases in 180 ms (none while dragging or with reduced motion) and the held knob grows to 1.06. Visual only, nothing is stored.
