Statistical emblems · styles A, B, C

A (default): a small white tile (56 px, radius 14, 0.5 px border), the drawing at 46 px, thin grey lines, fine hatching and one pink accent on the finding. B: no tile, the drawing at 54 px on the card, grey only (the accent becomes the main ink). C: the white tile, grey only; the finding is a solid dark fill instead of hatching plus accent, the rest turns grey. Each style is shown on the card colour at display size, then at 96 px. Strokes keep their pixel width at every size (non-scaling). In the prototype the Play bar switch "Emblems" picks the style (Studio only).
