## Looked at, not used

| Source | URL | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| Claybase (feustpat) | https://github.com/feustpat/claybase | Illustrations CC BY-NC 4.0, DALL-E generated, dark Catppuccin background, no chat motif |
| Jollof icons | https://github.com/gbmillz/jolloficons | MIT, but files are ~80 px and not clay-like |
| calvinsnax/soft-3d-icons, iTuer/soft-clay-icons | github.com | No licence file; no chat motif |
| Icons8 "Plasticine" / "Fluency" | img.icons8.com | Flat/outline, not 3D |
| IconScout #4403509, #5914397, #11102245, #2969412 | cdn3d.iconscout.com | Hard black offset shadow or flat card style; off-target |
| Figma Community clay sets (e.g. Saly, Clay 3D packs), Spline community | figma.com, spline.design | Need login |
| Freepik, Adobe Stock, Flaticon 3D | – | Login, paid or terms acceptance needed |
